How to play

  1. Add everyone's name, choose how many imposters and pick your categories.
  2. Pass the phone around. Each player holds their card to see the secret word — or that they're the imposter.
  3. The app picks who starts. Go round the group, each saying one word about the secret word.
  4. Imposters have to blend in without knowing the word. After a round or two, everyone votes on who's faking.
